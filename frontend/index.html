WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENS

Not a filter.
A full processing pipeline.

HALATION

Warm highlight bloom

The signature artifact of silver-halide film. Bright areas — windows, skin highlights, practicals — scatter a warm amber glow into surrounding shadows. Screen-blended at low opacity so it reads as optical, not added.

S-CURVE

Toe lift + shoulder rolloff

Film never clips highlights — it compresses them softly. Each grade preset applies a proper S-curve: blacks lift off pure black, highlights roll off instead of blowing. The result is contrast that reads as depth, not harshness.

FILM GRAIN

Spatially structured grain

Generated at half resolution then scaled back up with nearest-neighbour interpolation — this produces grain clumping that matches medium-ISO film, not the fine single-pixel pattern of digital noise. Luma-dominant, temporally varying.

MOTION

24fps with motion blur

Converts to 24fps and blends 18% of the previous frame in — simulating the trailing smear of a 180° film shutter. The result is the characteristic motion cadence of cinema, not the sharp choppiness of dropped frames.

OPTICS

Lens correction + diffusion

Compensates for iPhone wide-angle barrel distortion, then applies a gentle softening pass to remove the over-sharpened computational look that phone ISPs bake in. Shots feel like they came from a lens, not a sensor array.

AUDIO

Cinematic audio treatment

FFT-based noise reduction removes iPhone mic hiss, followed by a dynamic compressor and hard limiter. Dialogue and ambience sit at consistent levels without the brickwall compression of phone automatic loudness.

FAQ

Common questions.

Does it work on Android footage too?
Yes. Filmify accepts any H.264/HEVC MP4 or MOV, as well as AVCHD MTS files from digicams. The pipeline doesn't care what shot it — only the lens correction values are tuned for iPhone wide-angle distortion, so results may vary slightly on other cameras.
Will it change the aspect ratio or crop my footage?
No. Filmify never crops. Your original framing is preserved exactly — we only scale to 1080px wide while keeping the original proportions. What you shot is what you get back, graded.
How long does processing take?
Roughly 1–2 minutes per minute of source footage on a standard server. A 30-second iPhone clip typically finishes in under 90 seconds. Batch jobs run 2 clips at a time in parallel.
What file formats does it output?
H.264 MP4, 1080p, 9 Mbps — the standard format accepted by Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and every major platform. AAC audio at 192k.
Are my videos stored after processing?
Source files are deleted immediately after processing completes. Output files are served for download and then purged after 24 hours.
Can I use this for commercial work?
Yes — all plans include commercial use rights. There are no royalties or licensing restrictions on the output footage.
